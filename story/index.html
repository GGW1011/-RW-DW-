2009.02.10 · S4E16 Sariel 开始怀疑天堂 点开阅读

Uriel 和 Castiel 找到 Dean。Sariel 出现，问 Castiel：“Is he done?”

“Not yet; he’s on it.”

“Raphael is urging.”

“I know.”

这是 Sariel 第一次察觉到不对，开始产生反叛天堂的念头。她很庆幸 Castiel 也察觉了异常。Castiel 还不能像她一样直接说服自己反叛，但这已经足以让 Sariel 确认自己的判断。

Castiel：“Something is wrong with heaven.”

Sariel：“Then we need to find out.”

Castiel：“That’s not our business.”

Sariel：“Then just follow nonsense orders? Cas, it’s meaningless.”

Castiel：“I don’t know…”

Sariel：“It’s ok, I give you time to figure it out.”

Castiel 和 Uriel 对峙时，Uriel 说道：“No will, no wrath! No… god.” Sariel 使用天使之刃刺向 Uriel。

Sariel：“There’s still me.”

Sariel：“Am I late?”

Castiel：“A little bit…”

Sariel：“Don’t thank me; we’ve got to go.”

S4E20 与 Castiel 失联 点开阅读

S4E21 Castiel 放走 Sam 点开阅读

Castiel 放走了被 Dean 和 Bobby 关在地下室的 Sam。Sam 消失在转角后，Sariel 出现，看着 Castiel。

Sariel：“You sure that’s a good idea?”

Castiel：“Those are my orders.”

Sariel：“Orders? You’ve seen what he’s done.”

Castiel：“You should go…”

Sariel：“Don’t go too far.”

SEASON FIVE · THROUGH S5E21

S5E1 Michael 的容器 点开阅读

Zachariah 逼 Dean 成为 Michael 的容器。

Zachariah：“I just started.”

Sariel 和 Castiel 冲进来，杀死在场的所有天使，只留下 Zachariah。

Dean：“How do you…?”

Castiel：“Alive? Thank her.” Castiel 指向 Sariel。

Sariel 挥了挥手。

Sariel：“You scared, huh… Now, put these boys back together and go. I won’t ask twice.”

Zachariah 离开。

Castiel：“You two need to be more careful.”

Dean：“Who the hell is she…?”

Sariel 正要开口，Castiel 先解释：“She’s my sister; we were born together. Just like you two siblings.”

Sariel：“Exactly. Lucifer is circling his vessel; we are running out of time.”

Dean：“Cas, how did you?”

Dean 话还没问完，Sariel 和 Castiel 就已经离开了。

Dean：“These angels…”

S5E3 Sariel 找到 Castiel 点开阅读

S4 结尾，Raphael 杀死 Castiel。Cas 需要 Dean 帮忙找到他；God 也在人间。

Dean：“Where’s your sister?”

Castiel：“She’s not able to connect with me right now. If she’s here, other angels will know.”

Sariel：“Hi!”

Castiel：“Shit! What the….”

Sariel：“You know it’s really easy to track you, right?” Sariel 指了指 Castiel。

Castiel：“How…?”

Sariel：“Telepathy. Spooky, isn’t it?”

Dean 穿越到了 Lucifer 附身 Sam 的未来。2009年的 Dean 遇见了2014年的首领 Dean，首领 Dean 把他绑到了仓库。

2014 Dean：“I’m you.”

2009 Dean：“Name one thing people don’t know.”

2014 Dean：“Fine.”

Sariel 走进来，向 Dean 打招呼。

Sariel：“Oh, hey, babe.”

Dean：“Babe? Aren’t you Cas’s sister?”

Sariel：“Yeah, I’m your wife, man.”

Dean：“Wife?! Wait, can’t you just send me back?”

Sariel：“I wish, but I can’t change anything. I suggest you go check on Cas.”

2014 Dean：“Let’s go, baby.”

2009 Dean：“DICK!”

Dean 回到现实。

Dean：“Thanks, Cas.”

Castiel：“Thank her.”

Castiel：“We have an agreement.”

Dean：“You know… never mind. I didn’t know your name yet.”

Sariel：“Sariel.”

S5E13 · 2010 回到 1978 年 点开阅读

Castiel 把 Winchester 兄弟送回 1978 年，Sariel 也在那里。

Castiel：“She is in 1978.”

Dean：“What? Why 1978? I wasn’t even born yet.”

Castiel：“You won’t be… if she kills your parents.”

Dean：“What?”

Castiel：“Take others with me; it’s gonna weaken me.”

Sariel：“No worries. I can take one of them.”

Castiel：“Ready?”

Sariel：“Bend your knees.”

Sariel 和 Castiel 把他们带回 1978 年。

Sam：“Did we make it?”

Dean：“Unless they’re bringing pintos back into production.”

Sam 和 Dean 呼唤 Castiel。Castiel 醒来后，Sariel 确认他没事。

Castiel：“I’m fine. Better than I expected.”

Castiel 又昏了过去。

Dean：“He’s breathing… sort of.”

Sariel：“I’ll go take care of him. You boys can go; trust me.”

回到现在

Winchester 兄弟回来了。Sariel 把 Castiel 带回旅馆，Sam 也上前帮忙。

Sam：“We got you.”

Dean：“Son of a bitch, you guys made it!”

Sariel：“I need to rest. I’m exhausted.”

Castiel：“I… did? Surprise.” Castiel 随后又昏了过去。

Sam 和 Dean 把 Castiel 和 Sariel 扶到床上。

Dean：“Well, this is it.”

Sam：“This is what?”

Dean：“Team Free Will. One ex-blood junkie; one dropout with six bucks to his name, and two black-out angels. It’s awesome.”

S5E21 瘟疫之戒 点开阅读

医院

Castiel 给 Sam 和 Dean 打电话，Dean 接起电话。

Castiel：“Dean?”

Dean：“We all thought you were dead. Where the hell are you, man?”

Castiel：“A hospital. Sariel is here to take care of me.”

Dean：“Are you okay?”

Castiel：“No.”

Dean：“You want to elaborate?”

Castiel：“I just…”

Sariel：“Let me: you just woke up.”

Sariel：“The doctors are surprised; they thought he was brain-dead.”

Castiel 在 Van Nuys 之后，浑身是血、失去意识地出现在 Delacroix 附近的一艘捕虾船上。

Dean 告诉他们，Crowley 找到了打开 Satan’s box 的办法，想让他们赶过去。

Sariel 解释 Castiel 现在实际上是一个人类，她必须照顾他。她还要负责照看他、付这些杂七杂八的账单。

Castiel：“Hey. I’m not crap stuff.” Castiel 小声说道。

对付 Pestilence

Castiel 和 Sariel 赶去帮助 Winchester 兄弟。门被踹开，两位天使走了进来。

Dean：“How’d you guys get here?!”

Sariel：“We took a bus. Don’t worry, I…”

Castiel 突然吐血，跪倒在地上。

Sariel：“Hey.”

Pestilence：“Small angel…”

Sariel：“Shut up.”

Sariel 打了个响指，冻住 Pestilence；随后转身杀死他身边的恶魔。

Sariel：“Are you giving the ring or not?”

Pestilence：“You…”

Sariel：“I’ll take that as a yes.” Sariel 切下 Pestilence 戴着戒指的手指。

回到 Bobby 家

大家发现 Bobby 和 Crowley 做了交易。Sariel 用两根食指碰了碰，暗示她在问 Bobby 和 Crowley 是不是接吻了。

Bobby：“NO!!!”

Crowley 咳嗽了一声，拿出那张他亲吻 Bobby 的照片给大家看。

Sariel：“Why do you use a picture?”

Crowley：“Why do you use your tongue?”

所有人都用奇怪的表情看向 Bobby。

Sariel：“Okay, that’s enough.” Sariel 终于受不了了。
